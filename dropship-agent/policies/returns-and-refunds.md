## Faulty or not as described?

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you can reject a faulty item within **30 days** for a full refund. After that,
you're entitled to a repair or replacement, or in some cases a refund. We pay return postage for faulty items.
Contact {contact_email} with your order number and a photo of the problem.
