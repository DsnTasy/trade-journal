# Policy templates

Run `python run.py policies` to fill these in with your details from `brand.yaml` (section `business`).
The filled copies go to `output/policies/`. Search them for **[CHECK** and fix every one, then paste them into
Shopify: Settings → Policies (privacy, refund, shipping), and link them in your footer.

They're plain-English starting points based on UK rules (UK GDPR, Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013,
Consumer Rights Act 2015). They are **not legal advice**. If in doubt, use a solicitor or a reputable
policy-generator service.
